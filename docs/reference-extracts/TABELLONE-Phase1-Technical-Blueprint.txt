Executive SummaryPhase 1 delivers an Owner Operating System: a multi-tenant web platform where AdSpace Owners manage inventory, clients, bookings, availability, and invoices — with Admin oversight and Advertiser account foundations — without a public marketplace or payment gateway.Recommended architecture: Modular monolith with clear domain boundaries, API-first design, PostgreSQL, and a path to extract services in Phase 2/3.2.
