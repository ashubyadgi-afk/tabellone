TABELLONEPHASE 1 – PLATFORM FOUNDATION + IMDDetailed Product & Technical Development Plan for Cursor1. Phase 1 VisionPhase 1 establishes the foundation of TABELLONE and delivers the Inventory Management Dashboard (IMD). The primary business goal is to onboard AdSpace Owners early and give them a useful digital system to manage their advertising inventory, clients, bookings and invoices, even before the full public marketplace is launched.2. Phase 1 Business OutcomeAt the end of Phase 1, TABELLONE should be usable as an Owner Operating System:Owner registers → Owner is verified → Owner logs in → Creates AdSpaces → Manages inventory → Creates client bookings → Tracks availability → Generates invoices.3. Important Data-Model PrincipleSeparate internal inventory from public marketplace listing.Example: an Owner may have 100 AdSpaces in IMD, but only 30 may eventually be publicly listed. Therefore, 'AdSpace exists in IMD' and 'AdSpace is publicly listed' must not be the same flag or state.22.
